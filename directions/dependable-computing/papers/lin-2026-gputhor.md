### Summary and evidence

The GPUThor arXiv v1 abstract and author project report multi-bit errors and exploits from non-uniform accesses on tested ECC-enabled NVIDIA GDDR6 configurations. It extends the GPUHammer line from fault generation to limits of the evaluated ECC protection.

### Place in the seminar

- Citation-only: qualify ECC as a system-specific protection with a finite correction capability.
- Provide the recent GPU counterpart to non-uniform-pattern background without expanding the deep-reading list.
